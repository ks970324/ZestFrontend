Game rule

1. Use the arrow keys to move your character.

2. Press the Enter key to perform actions (ending movement or attacking).

3. Each player has five hearts of health. Reduce your opponent's hearts to zero to win.

4. Damage rules for attacks:

  • If the distance between players is 0–1 tile: deduct 1.5 hearts.
  • If the distance is 2 tiles: deduct 1 heart.
  • If the distance is 3–4 tiles: deduct 0.5 heart.
